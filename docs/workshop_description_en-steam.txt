[i]“Destroy, and heal.”[/i]

The Arknights operator [b]Mon3tr[/b] enters the Constant. Wield Reconstruction against your enemies, protect your allies with bouncing heals, and leap into the enemy ranks in Meltdown form when it matters most.

[b]Melee damage · Bouncing heals · Meltdown assault[/b]

[b]Before playing, subscribe to and enable the required mod: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3677284770]Arknights: Nexus[/url].[/b]

[b]Bouncing Heals and Tactical Synergy[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Bouncing Heals:[/b] Prioritize targets with the lowest Health percentage, then jump to other allies. Each subsequent bounce restores less Health.
[*][b]Tactical Synergy:[/b] From Elite 1 onward, bouncing heals grant healed units a brief attack speed boost.
[/list]

[h3]Stratagem: Hyperpressurized Link[/h3]
[b]Available from the start · Automatic activation[/b]

Once enough Skill Points have been accumulated, the next attack hit triggers a [b]bouncing heal[/b]. Stay in the fight to provide periodic healing for yourself and nearby allies.

[h3]Stratagem: Overload[/h3]
[b]Unlocked at Elite 1 · Manual activation[/b]

For a period after activation, [b]every attack hit[/b] triggers a bouncing heal and strengthens the attack speed bonus granted by Tactical Synergy.

[b]Stacks with Hyperpressurized Link[/b] for stronger healing, helping your team handle prolonged fights.

[h3]Stratagem: Meltdown[/h3]
[b]Unlocked at Elite 2 · Manual activation · Select a landing point[/b]

Leap to the selected location, strike nearby enemies on landing, and unequip your handheld item to enter [b]Meltdown form[/b].

[list]
[*][b]Enhanced Form:[/b] Greatly increases maximum Health and boosts damage, attack speed, and attack range.
[*][b]Area True Damage:[/b] Melee attacks deal true damage to surrounding enemies.
[*][b]Fight to Survive:[/b] Health drains continuously, while successful attacks restore Health based on Mon3tr's base attack power.
[/list]

When the skill ends or Mon3tr is close to death, Mon3tr returns near the starting point. A brief period of [b]protection against death[/b] follows, so watch your remaining Health and heal promptly.

[b]Restrictions:[/b] Cannot be activated while riding. Handheld equipment and riding are unavailable during Meltdown.

[h2]Equipment and Charging[/h2]

[h3]Reconstruction[/h3]

Mon3tr's starting weapon. [b]More charge means stronger attacks[/b], and the blade's glow changes with its charge level. Durability represents its charge; normal attacks do not consume charge. When wielded by Mon3tr, it deals [b]true damage[/b].

[list]
[*][b]Hunger Charging:[/b] While equipped, slowly consumes Hunger when it is above 40 to charge the weapon up to 80%.
[*][b]Healing Charging:[/b] Once the weapon reaches 80%, its wielder can receive Mon3tr's bouncing heals to charge it further toward full capacity.
[*][b]Meltdown Boost:[/b] If Reconstruction is equipped when Meltdown begins, a small amount of charge is consumed to further increase attack power for that transformation.
[/list]

Stay well fed before a fight and keep healing during combat to build Reconstruction's power.

[h3]M3 Cocoon Armor[/h3]

Bouncing heals also [b]restore durability to M3 Cocoon Armor[/b] worn by the healed unit, keeping equipment in good condition as allies recover.

See the required Arknights: Nexus mod's description for the armor's other effects.
